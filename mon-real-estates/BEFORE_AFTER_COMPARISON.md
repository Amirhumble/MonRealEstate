## 🎓 Lessons Learned

### What Works

✅ **Large Hero Images** - Immediate visual impact  
✅ **Status Badges** - Quick information  
✅ **Key Metrics Cards** - Scannable data  
✅ **Sticky Sidebar** - Always accessible contact  
✅ **Image Gallery** - Engaging exploration  
✅ **Structured Sections** - Clear organization  
✅ **Agent Cards** - Personal connection  
✅ **Related Properties** - Extended engagement  

### What to Avoid

❌ **Information Overload** - Keep it organized  
❌ **Slow Loading** - Optimize images  
❌ **Hidden CTAs** - Make contact obvious  
❌ **Poor Mobile Experience** - Test thoroughly  
❌ **Generic Content** - Be specific  
❌ **Cluttered Layout** - Use white space
